I am a PhD Researcher at Queen Mary University of London, supervised by [Prof. Ioannis Patras](https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~ioannisp/) and in collaboration with [Tavus](https://www.tavus.io/). My research focuses on deep generative models and multimodal learning for affective computing.

Previously, I was a Senior AI Developer at the PwC Italy AI Center of Excellence, where I led the development of production-scale multimodal video generation systems and worked on multimodal perception and Agentic AI systems and protocols. I hold an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Florence (2024) and have published at venues including ICCV and ECCV, earning a Best Workshop Paper Award at ECCV 2024.
